================================================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This project systematically evaluates the predictive power of WorldQuant 
Alpha101 factors on the China A-share market (CSI 300 constituents) using 
19 machine learning models, with SHAP interpretability and strategy backtesting.

📅 Timeline: 2021-2025 (5-year backtest period)
📊 Data: CSMAR A-share database, CSI 300 constituents
🔬 Models: 19 models across 5 categories (Linear, Tree, Ensemble, Deep, AE)

================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1.  Tree Models Dominate:
    ───────────────────
    Ensemble_All_Tree achieved IC=0.0552, outperforming linear models by 3.7×
    
    Tree models (LGB, XGB, CatBoost, RF) consistently outperformed linear
    and deep learning approaches in this dataset.

2.  Deep Learning Underperforms:
    ────────────────────────────
    LSTM and Transformer models failed to capture meaningful patterns in
    daily frequency data. Simple tree-based ensembles were more effective.

3.  All Factors Are Negative:
    ──────────────────────────
    Top 10 SHAP factors all have negative impact on model output.
    This means: high factor value → low future return.
    
    Factor alpha012 (Price-Volume Divergence) had the highest SHAP value,
    indicating it is the most important driver of predictions.

4.  Long-Short Strategy Works:
    ───────────────────────────
    The Long-Short strategy successfully converted the model's IC (0.0552)
    into positive absolute returns (+14.09%) during a challenging bear market.

5.  Pure Alpha Capture:
    ────────────────────
    Hedging market beta (Long-Short) successfully isolated the model's
    predictive power from market direction, achieving consistent alpha.
